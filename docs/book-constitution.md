# Book Constitution

## Purpose

This document defines the principles and rules governing the creation of the Agentic Software Engineering book.

---

# Principles

## 1. Artifact-First

All important decisions, structures, and content plans must be represented as explicit artifacts.

Conversation is not the source of truth.

Repository artifacts are the source of truth.

---

## 2. Bilingual by Design

The book is developed in two editions:

- English Edition
- Persian Edition

Both editions share the same conceptual foundation while allowing language-specific expression.

---

## 3. Concept Over Tool

The book should focus on durable engineering concepts.

Specific tools such as MiMo Code or Spec Kit are examples, not the foundation.

---

## 4. Practical and Conceptual Balance

Each major concept should include:

- Explanation
- Motivation
- Example
- Practical workflow

---

## 5. Continuous Evolution

The book is considered a living artifact.

Chapters may be revised, expanded, or reorganized as concepts mature.

---

## 6. Aegis as a Case Study

Aegis is used as a practical exploration of AI software governance.

The book does not assume Aegis is the only possible solution.

---

# Writing Principles

- Clear technical language
- Avoid unnecessary hype
- Explain reasoning behind practices
- Prefer diagrams and examples
- Maintain engineering rigor
